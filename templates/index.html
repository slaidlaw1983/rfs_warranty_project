Property Parameters

Shock Parameters

Life multiplier <1 shortens design life; cost multiplier >1 increases costs. Defaults model a moderate stress scenario (20% shorter life, 30% higher costs).

e.g. 0.80 = 20% shorter life
e.g. 1.30 = 30% cost increase

Financial Parameters

Used for the year-by-year cash flow projection (separate from the Monte Carlo stress test).

e.g. 0.03 = 3% annual cost inflation
e.g. 0.025 = 2.5% on reserve balance
