Variability Parameters

The engineer's current cost and design life are treated as the P50 (median) for each component. Each Monte Carlo trial draws a random cost (lognormal, mean=1.0) and design life (normal, mean=1.0) per component. Standard deviation defaults to 0.30 (≈30% variability — captures realistic cost overruns and component-life uncertainty).
